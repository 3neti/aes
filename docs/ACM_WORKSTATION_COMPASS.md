# ACM workstation compass

The officer workstation serves a single precinct and supports clear, deliberate actions. Voter tablets should remain focused on private ballot entry. One officer-issued number follows the voter to printing; readiness must not print or consume it. Never display ballot selections to the officer. Reuse established election operations and make interim results and printer outcomes understandable.

The **ballot** is the digital record of the voter’s selections. The **voter-verifiable paper audit trail (VVPAT)** is the printed record the voter checks. Use these terms consistently in voter and officer instructions. Distinguish a file prepared or a job sent to the printer from confirmed physical printing.

## Acceptance checklist

- Home opens voter entry; voters cannot self-issue a number in this flow.
- Officer can issue a number, check VVPAT readiness, explicitly print, and access interim returns.
- Ten independent booth sessions can use officer-issued numbers.
- Readiness checks leave the ballot available and printing revalidates it.
- Errors are visible, and interim returns identify their precinct and snapshot time.
- Existing secondary demo tools remain accessible.
